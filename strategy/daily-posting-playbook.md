# Kenji & Yuki: Daily Posting Playbook (TikTok + Instagram)

Goal: grow two AI Shiba characters, **Kenji** and **Yuki**, into a recognizable franchise fast, then monetize.
Treat it like a sitcom with a cast, not a pet page.

---

## 1. Positioning: build a *universe*, not a channel

| | Kenji | Yuki | Together |
|---|---|---|---|
| Energy | Confident, stylish, deadpan reactions | Playful sass, side-eyes, Pilates + matcha | Chemistry, bickering, teamwork |
| Signature move | The slow "unimpressed" head turn | The side-eye + hair flip | Synchronized reaction / stare at camera |
| Best topics | Work, gym, dating as a guy, money | Wellness, fashion, friends, dating as a girl | Couple fights, roommates, coworkers, "him vs her" |

- **Name each one on screen** in the first few seconds of early videos ("Kenji when…") so viewers learn who they are.
- **Give each one a catchphrase / repeatable moment.** Repeats build recognition. Same face, same expression, same sound cue.
- **Bring in side characters slowly** (a boss, Yuki's best friend, a rival Shiba) so you have more storylines later.
- Outfits change every scene, faces never change. Keep **locked reference images / character sheets** in Higgsfield and use them every time you generate.

## 2. Formats (rotate these)

1. **Viral remake** (your main growth engine): copy a trending video beat for beat with Kenji/Yuki. Keep the original joke and timing. Speed matters: post within **24–72 h** of the trend peaking.
2. **POV / relatable skit**: "POV: your coworker says 'quick call'". Short and easy to share.
3. **Him vs Her**: split screen, Kenji vs Yuki doing the same thing. Gets comments and shares.
4. **Series episodes**: "Yuki's Pilates era, Part 3". Gives people a reason to follow.
5. **Day in the life**: Yuki's matcha morning, Kenji's office day. Slower, sets the mood, good for Instagram.
6. **Comment reply videos**: answer a top comment *in character*. Good engagement and gives you free ideas.

## 3. Daily cadence

**Phase 1: Testing (weeks 1–3)**
- **TikTok: 2–3 posts/day** (morning, lunch, evening in your audience's time zone).
- **Instagram Reels: 1–2/day**, plus 3–5 Stories (polls, behind-the-scenes, "who's right, Kenji or Yuki?").
- Each video should be 7–20 s. Short videos with high completion rates are how a new account gets distributed.
- Upload clean files to each platform separately. **Never repost a TikTok with its watermark to Instagram**, because Instagram pushes it down.

**Phase 2: Doubling down (weeks 4–8)**
- Keep 2/day minimum. Spend 70% of your output on the formats/characters that are working, 20% on variations of them, 10% on new experiments.
- Start making a few longer videos (60 s+) on TikTok once you have hits. Longer videos are what count toward Creator Rewards payouts.

**Phase 3: Franchise (month 3+)**
- Recurring weekly series, collabs/duets with other AI animal accounts, brand integrations.

### Daily routine (about 2–3 h once it's running smoothly)
| Time | Task |
|---|---|
| 15 min | **Trend scan**: TikTok For You + Creative Center, Reels tab, trending sounds. Save 3–5 candidates. |
| 15 min | **Pick + script**: for each pick, write down the original beats (hook, setup, punchline, timestamps) and cast Kenji, Yuki or both. |
| 60–90 min | **Generate** in Higgsfield using the locked character references; edit to the original timing; add on-screen text. |
| 10 min | **Post** at your scheduled times with caption, hashtags and a pinned comment. |
| 20 min | **Engage**: reply in character during the first hour; pick one comment to answer with a video tomorrow. |
| 10 min | **Log metrics** (see section 6). |

Keep a **buffer of 5–10 evergreen videos** (work/love/everyday skits that don't depend on a trend) so you never miss a day.

## 4. The first 2 seconds decide everything

- **Visual hook first frame**: a character's face mid-expression (side-eye, shock, deadpan). Never open on an empty scene.
- **On-screen text hook**: "Kenji when HR says 'we're a family'", "Yuki when he says 'I'm 5 min away'".
- **Use the trending sound** from the platform's own library, so the video gets grouped with that trend and you avoid copyright problems.
- **Loopable endings**: end on a frame that flows back into the start, which pushes rewatch rate up.
- Captions short, plus 1 question: "Who's right? 👀". Use 3–5 hashtags: 1–2 broad (#shiba #funnyanimals), 1–2 niche (#officehumor #pilatesgirl), 1 brand tag (#KenjiAndYuki).

Viral-remake slots go on top of this every day, whenever a trend fits.

## 6. Measure and adapt

Track per video in a simple sheet: date, platform, format, characters, trend/sound, views at 24 h, **average watch %**, **shares**, saves, follows gained.

- **Shares and watch %** matter more than likes. Make more of whatever gets shared.
- A video that gets well past your usual views → make **2–3 follow-ups within 48 h** (Part 2, the other character's version, a reaction).
- A format that flops 5 times in a row → drop it.
- Check which character drives more follows (Kenji solo, Yuki solo, or duo) and adjust the mix each week.

## 7. Engagement tactics

- Reply in character voice ("Kenji has read your comment and is choosing to ignore it 😐").
- Pin a comment that starts a debate ("Team Kenji or Team Yuki?").
- Use polls/question stickers in Stories; turn the answers into videos.
- Duet/stitch other viral animal content as Kenji/Yuki reacting.
- Consistent profile: both faces in the avatar, bio says what the account is ("Kenji & Yuki 🐕 two Shibas, one braincell. New episode daily"), link in bio for merch/newsletter later.

## 8. Monetization path

1. **Platform payouts**: TikTok Creator Rewards (follower and view thresholds, videos over 1 min), Instagram bonuses where your country has them. Check the current eligibility rules in each app, since they change.
2. **Brand deals**: matcha, Pilates studios/apparel, pet brands, coffee, fashion and office/productivity apps match the characters perfectly. Make a 1-page media kit once you have a few viral hits.
3. **TikTok Shop / affiliate**: Yuki's matcha set, Kenji's outfits.
4. **Merch and stickers**: Kenji's deadpan face and Yuki's side-eye are natural sticker/meme products.
5. **Licensing**: animated characters with an audience can be licensed for ads.

## 9. Rules to protect the account

- **Label AI content.** Both TikTok and Instagram require AI-generated realistic content to be labeled. Turn on the AI-generated toggle. Unlabeled AI content can be suppressed or removed.
- **Recreate, don't reupload.** Remake the format and joke with your own generated footage. Don't reuse the original creator's footage. Credit the original creator in the caption when the remake is close to their video.
- Use sounds from the in-app libraries (commercial library for branded posts).
- Don't buy followers or use engagement pods, because they damage distribution.
- Warm up new accounts: a few days of normal scrolling/engaging in the niche before and while posting.
- Keep a consistent upload rhythm. Long gaps hurt more than one weaker post.
