## The original's joke (transcribed)
1. She puts money under a metal bowl on the kitchen island: "If you can get the money from under this bowl without touching the bowl, you can keep it." / "I can't touch the bowl?" / "Nope."
2. He: "Back up, give me some space." Does a goofy fake magic ritual over the bowl ("walla walla walla... bam!").
3. "Done. Check it." She lifts the bowl herself: **the money is gone**.
4. She's stunned, turns the bowl over, looks inside: "What did you do?! How?" He: "A magician never reveals his secrets."
5. She keeps staring at the empty bowl, baffled. Hook text: "When your wife tries to prank you back..."
