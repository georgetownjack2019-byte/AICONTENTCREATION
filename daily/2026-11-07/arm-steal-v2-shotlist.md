# "POV: his arm is officially mine": v2 shot list (funnier, cuter, more dramatic)

Feedback on v1: not as funny or cute as the original; Kenji's arm wasn't in Yuki's hold at the start; Yuki looked too awake.

## Fixes
- **Cuter:** rounder faces, bigger glossy eyes, chubbier cheeks, small paws. The prompts ask for "extra cute, plush, big sparkly eyes" while keeping the reference faces.
- **Yuki asleep the whole time** until the eye-pop. Even her grab is done half-asleep, which is funnier.
- **The setup shows what she's losing:** she starts **hugging Kenji's arm like a teddy bear**.
- **Exaggerated, dramatic acting:** big grumpy pout, eyes popping to huge saucers, an aggressive yank that *flips Kenji like a pancake*.
- **Sound design carries the jokes** (see the timing table).

## Shots (~15 s)
| # | Time | Shot | Action | Sound | Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–3 s | High angle, both in bed | Yuki asleep, **hugging Kenji's arm like a teddy**, tiny smile; Kenji snoring | lullaby + cute snore | Animated |
| 2 | 3–5 s | Same | Kenji snorts, rolls away and **yanks his arm out of her hug** | sheet rustle, snore stops | Animated |

| 6 | 12–15.5 s | Medium, both | Kenji blinks awake, confused, sees his arm trapped, **deadpan stare at camera**, big sigh, then gives in with a soft smile | defeated sigh, lullaby ends | Animated |
