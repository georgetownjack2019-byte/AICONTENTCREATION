# Kenji & Yuki — Character Bible

Two expressive anthropomorphic Shiba Inus. **Not a couple account.** Each one carries
videos solo, and they also team up as a duo (coworkers, roommates, friends, rivals,
dating, whatever the joke needs).

## Official look
**Master reference:** `refs/kenji_yuki_master_ref.jpg` (the user's original Grok design).
Higgsfield media ID: `085076e9-c39d-4071-9d11-7ca444a3cd06`. Attach it as the image
reference on every generation.

## Visual style
Stylized **3D cartoon**, animated-feature-film look (the "orange cat video" style):
plush chunky bodies, slightly oversized heads, big glossy expressive eyes, very readable
cream eyebrow-dot "brows", soft stylized fur, warm cinematic lighting. Never photoreal.
Vertical 9:16 framing.

## Rule of consistency
- **Locked (never changes):** face shape, red-orange coat, cream *urajiro* (cheeks,
  muzzle, chest), cream eyebrow dots, pointed ears, curled fluffy tail, eye shape,
  plush 3D render style, body proportions.
- **Free (changes every video):** outfit, shoes, accessories, hair *styling*, props, setting.
