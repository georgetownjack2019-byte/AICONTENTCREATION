## Upload checklist

### TikTok (phone app)
1. **+** → Upload → pick the mp4.
2. **Sounds:** leave the original sound on (adjust volume only if adding a trending sound).
3. **Next** → paste the caption.
4. **Edit cover** → pick the frame closest to the cover image (TikTok uses a frame from the video) and add the cover text in the app.
5. **More options → turn ON "AI-generated content"** (required for AI videos).
6. Allow comments, Duet and Stitch (more reach).
7. Post at the slot time → pin the comment → reply to the first comments within the first hour.

### Instagram Reels (phone app)
1. **+** → Reel → pick the mp4 → **Next** (skip extra edits; text is already burned in).
2. **Edit cover** → **Add from camera roll** → choose the cover jpg (keep the title in the middle so the grid crop shows it).
3. Paste the caption.
4. **Advanced settings → turn ON "AI label" / "Add AI info"**.
5. Share to Feed: ON.
6. Post → pin the comment → reply to comments in the first hour.

### After posting
- Don't delete or repost a slow video in the first 24 h.
- Next day, log views, average watch %, shares and follows in `daily/performance.md`.
