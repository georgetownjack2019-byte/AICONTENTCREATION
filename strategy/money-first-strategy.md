# Money-first strategy (built on viral remakes)

No strategy can *guarantee* virality. What this plan does is stack the odds: ride formats that are
already proven, post enough volume to get lucky often, and keep production cost per video low so every
hit is profitable.

## 1. Unit economics: know your cost per video
| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Daily test clip (Seedance 2.0 Fast, 720p, 8 s) | **20 credits** |
| Premium clip for proven hits (Seedance 2.5, 720p, 8 s) | 56 credits |
| 2 test clips/day for 30 days | ~1,200 credits/month |

Current balance: ~104 credits (Plus plan), which is enough for about 4 test clips. **Before switching the
daily run on, make sure your monthly credits cover 2 clips/day (~1,200), or start at 1/day.** Spend
premium credits only on formats that have already worked once.

## 2. Pick trends that make money, not just views
Score each trend on **shareability**, not just humour. The money comes from:
1. **Follows** (they lead to Creator Rewards eligibility and brand-deal rate cards). Recurring characters + series turn views into follows. Always cast Kenji/Yuki recognisably and end on a signature move.
2. **Shares/tags**: "tag your boyfriend", "send to your coworker". Love and work trends get tagged most → prioritise them.
3. **Watch time**: TikTok Creator Rewards pays only for videos **over 1 minute**. Once a short remake hits, stitch 6–8 clips into a 60–75 s "Kenji & Yuki compilation" or a mini-episode. That is the main platform-payout lever.

## 3. Viral remake rules (to get a strong start)
- Use **Fresh/Rising** trends only. Post within **24–72 h**. Speed beats polish.
- **Keep the original beat structure and timing**, swap the people for Kenji/Yuki, add one character twist (Kenji's deadpan, Yuki's side-eye) at the end.
- **Recreate, never reupload** the original creator's footage. Use the trending sound from the in-app library.
- Label as AI-generated on both apps.

## 5. Weekly money review (Sunday, 15 min)
From `daily/performance.md`:
- Top 2 videos by **shares** → make Part 2 / other-character version (premium model allowed).
- Format with highest **follows per 1k views** → raise its share of the daily mix.
- Anything with < 50% average watch time 3 times → drop.
