# Format: "What should we be for Halloween?" (proven, user's first remake)

Source: `kenji_yuki_costume_remake_v4_preview.mp4` (60 s, 9:16, made with Grok).

## Structure
1. **Setup:** Yuki, phone in paw, pitches a couples costume in the cozy living room.
2. **Ping-pong:** quick alternating single-character shots, each reacting.
3. **Cutaway gag:** every suggestion cuts to a white-background insert of that
   character in the costume (maid, flour-covered, Rapunzel, frying pan, mascot, Medusa…).
4. **Reaction beats:** Kenji's deadpan / shocked face sells each bad idea.
5. **Button:** Yuki gets the last word with an excited grin.

## Production notes
- Word-by-word white captions at mid-frame (bold, black outline).
- One consistent set (living room, TV, pumpkins, fairy lights) for every dialogue shot.
- Inserts are separate stills on white: cheap to generate, and they carry the jokes.
- Reuse this skeleton for other "couple debates": what to eat, where to travel, matching
  tattoos, baby names, holiday plans.
