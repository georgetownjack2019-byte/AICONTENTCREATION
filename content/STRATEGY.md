# Content Strategy

## Format: animal remakes of viral videos
Take a trending/viral video (work, love, everyday life), recast it with Kenji and/or Yuki,
and **keep the original joke, audio and timing intact.** Viewers already know the
format, so recognition + cute animal = shares.

### Rules for a remake
1. Keep the original audio/sound (use TikTok/IG trending sounds natively when posting).
2. Match the beat timing: same cuts, same pause before the punchline.
3. Put the joke in the on-screen text in the first 1 second (hook).
4. 7–15 seconds is the sweet spot; loopable endings boost watch time.
5. Remake the *format*, not the creator's actual footage, likeness or branding.

## Content pillars
| Pillar | Who | Example premises |
|---|---|---|
| Work life | Kenji solo, Yuki solo, both | "When the meeting could've been an email", "Me pretending to work when the boss walks by", Monday vs Friday energy |
| Love & dating | Both | "When he says 'I'm 5 minutes away'", "Her: are you mad? Me:", texting-back timing |
| Everyday life | Either | Matcha order drama, Pilates class reality, roommate fights over the thermostat |
| Yuki-verse | Yuki | Pilates girl starter pack, "that girl" morning routine gone wrong |
| Kenji-verse | Kenji | Gym bro logic, outfit check confidence, "rate my fit" |

## Posting cadence (starting point)
- 1–2 posts/day on TikTok, cross-post to Instagram Reels.
- Alternate: solo Kenji → solo Yuki → duo.
- Track: 3-second hold rate, completion %, shares. Double down on whichever pillar wins.

## Production pipeline (Higgsfield)
1. Pick a trending format and write a beat sheet (shot, action, text, timing).
2. Generate each shot's keyframe image from the character sheet reference (new outfit per scene).
3. Animate the keyframes into video clips (image-to-video / motion control).
4. Assemble, add trending sound + captions, and run the virality predictor before posting.

## Monetisation path
TikTok Creator Rewards (needs 10k followers and 1-minute+ videos) · IG Reels bonuses ·
brand deals (matcha, athleisure, pet, coffee and productivity apps fit the characters) ·
merch/stickers once the characters are recognisable.
