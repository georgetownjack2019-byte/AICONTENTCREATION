# Kenji & Yuki — Character Bible

Two expressive anthropomorphic Shiba Inus. **Not a couple account.** Each one carries
videos solo, and they also team up as a duo (coworkers, roommates, friends, rivals,
dating, whatever the joke needs).

## Rule of consistency
- **Locked (never changes):** face shape, coat colour, markings, eye shape, ear shape,
  overall render style.
- **Free (changes every scene):** outfit, hairstyle/hair accessories, props, jewellery,
  setting.
- Always generate from the approved character-sheet image as the identity reference.
